# Other games

Rock 'n' Roller is one of a family of small browser games. Most of them take a
character asset from another repository, as it is, and build a game around it.
These are the others.

## The rolling games

These share an engine. Madness came first, and Dungeon Roller and everything
after it are built on it.

- **[Madness](https://github.com/h1ddenpr0cess20/madness)**: a Marble
  Madness-style 3D racer. You roll a glass marble down courses that hang in the
  dark, racing a single clock, past hammers, acid and black steelies. The
  marble is [Alan](https://github.com/h1ddenpr0cess20/alan)'s eye.
- **[Dungeon Roller](https://github.com/h1ddenpr0cess20/dungeon-roller)**: a
  rolling dungeon crawler. You roll a d20 through twelve torchlit dungeons.
  The die is your party of four heroes, and whatever number is on top when you
  hit a monster is the roll you fight with. The die is
  [Nat](https://github.com/h1ddenpr0cess20/nat)'s d20. Rock 'n' Roller uses
  this game's engine.
- **[Catroller](https://github.com/h1ddenpr0cess20/catroller)**: a sneaky
  rolling game. You're a ball of red yarn loose in a house at night, trying to
  reach the cat flap past six cats. Mice, flies and beetles keep the cats busy,
  until you scare one off. The yarn is
  [Carol](https://github.com/h1ddenpr0cess20/carol)'s.
- **[Blubber](https://github.com/h1ddenpr0cess20/blubber)**: a casual haunted
  maze game. Blubber, a blueberry ghost, floats through ten mazes from
  Halloween to midwinter, lighting lanterns to open the moon gate and
  collecting candy. Nothing can end a run.

## The egg games

Each one stars [Marc](https://github.com/h1ddenpr0cess20/marc)'s egg, unchanged
and always upright.

- **[Eggscape the Matrix](https://github.com/h1ddenpr0cess20/eggscape)**: a 3D
  runner-platformer down a self-building wireframe course. Jump the holes, dodge
  the agents and collect the bits.
- **[Eggscape the Farm](https://github.com/h1ddenpr0cess20/eggscape-ii)**: the
  sequel, set in a lit afternoon field. Hop the ditches, dodge the bales and
  pull up what's growing.
- **[Eggscape the Permanent
  Underclass](https://github.com/h1ddenpr0cess20/eggscape-iii)**: a walkway
  forty storeys over a surveillance city. There's no getting out of this one,
  so the score is how long you last.
- **[Runny Egg](https://github.com/h1ddenpr0cess20/runny-egg)**: a hurdles
  race for six eggs in five lanes. You aren't the fastest, but you have to
  finish in one piece, and three cracks ends the heat.

## The rest

- **[Short Order](https://github.com/h1ddenpr0cess20/short-order)**: a cooking
  game in one cast-iron pan. You take one ticket at a time: potato & egg scramble, scrambled
  eggs, sunny side up, over easy, French or diner omelettes. You dice, beat,
  season and fry each one on a gas burner that starts cold.
- **[Neverending Tunnel](https://github.com/h1ddenpr0cess20/tunnel)**: a
  first-person runner toward the light at the end of the tunnel, which you
  never reach. The tunnel is ray traced in a WebGPU shader. You jump pits and
  slide under beams.
- **[Tetrisnake 3D](https://github.com/h1ddenpr0cess20/tetrisnake-3d)**: Snake
  and Tetris mashed together in a 3D well, rendered with three.js and WebGPU.
  You steer the snake in six directions to eat, then choose where it lands. It
  started as a joke about AI game mashups.
- **[Tetrisnake](https://github.com/h1ddenpr0cess20/tetrisnake)**: the original
  2D version, first written in Pygame and then for the web.
- **[Capitol Quest](https://github.com/h1ddenpr0cess20/capitol-quest)**: a
  work-in-progress satirical RPG on the National Mall, where the Victory
  Engine can only report success.
